### Project Description

A highly advanced system that calculates exactly how disappointed your parents are in you based on your everyday life choices. From exam marks to waking up at 11 AM, every decision contributes to your final **Parent Disappointment Score™**.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Parents have always been disappointed, but there has never been a scientifically accurate way to measure **HOW disappointed** they actually are.

Is it a mild *“Hmm.”* or a devastating *“Look at Sharma ji's son”*?

We decided this extremely important problem needed solving.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

The **Parent Disappointment Calculator** asks users a series of completely unnecessary questions about their life and calculates their **Disappointment Percentage**.

The system then generates an official-looking **Parent Disappointment Report**, including:

* Current disappointment level
* Main causes of disappointment
* Comparison with imaginary Sharma ji's son
* Estimated number of *“When I was your age...”* speeches
* Probability of receiving the question **“What are you doing with your life?”**
* Final parental verdict
